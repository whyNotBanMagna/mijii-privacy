应用名称：蜜迹（Mijii）

欢迎使用蜜迹。本政策说明我们如何对待你在使用本应用时涉及的信息。蜜迹的核心设计是：回忆与照片默认只保存在你的设备上，我们不运营用于同步你们相册的云端业务服务器。

1. 我们收集哪些信息？

1.1 本机处理、不上传给我们的内容

你在应用内创建或导入的以下内容，保存在设备本地（应用沙盒与本地数据库），不会自动上传到我们的服务器（我们也不提供此类业务后端）：

  • 情侣昵称、在一起的日期
  • 回忆日期、地点 / 主题、心情标记、文字笔记
  • 你选择收进蜜迹的照片及其缩略图
  • 特殊日设置、本地提醒开关
  • 语言偏好等界面设置

卸载应用或在系统设置中清除应用数据，通常会删除上述本地内容（除非你事先导出了备份）。

1.2 你主动发送的反馈

若你使用「一起变好」并通过系统邮件应用写信，邮件内容、发件邮箱与设备邮件服务会按你邮件服务商的规则处理。我们仅在收到邮件后阅读你自愿写下的内容，不会因此获得你们相册中的照片访问权限。

1.3 备份与分享

「备份与导出」会在你的操作下生成包含全部数据库与照片的 zip。文件保存在你指定或系统分享面板选择的位置（例如文件 App、隔空投送）。是否离开本机，由你决定；我们不会代为上传到蜜迹云端。

若你使用「分享」功能，蜜迹仅在设备上生成海报图片。你可通过系统分享、保存到相册，或（在完成开发者配置后）Facebook 官方分享对话框把图片发给 Facebook / Instagram 等应用。蜜迹不会代发，也不会把分享图上传到我们的服务器；你发到第三方平台后，适用该平台的隐私政策。应用内可能链接 Facebook SDK，仅用于你主动触发的分享对话框，并已关闭自动事件与广告标识采集；不作分析或广告用途。

1.4 崩溃诊断与使用分析（Firebase）

为修复闪退与了解功能使用情况，应用集成了 Google Firebase：

  • Crashlytics：崩溃堆栈、设备型号、操作系统版本、应用版本等诊断信息。
  • Analytics：匿名/伪匿名的应用打开、保存回忆、分享海报、备份导出、评分询问等事件名（不含昵称、笔记正文、照片内容或文件路径）。

上述数据由 Google 代表我们处理，传输使用加密通道；我们不用其投放个性化广告，也不出售你们的回忆与照片。详见 Firebase 隐私与安全。

1.5 我们不收集的内容

我们不为广告画像或跨 App 追踪收集：

  • 广告标识符用于追踪（不为此请求 ATT / 广告 ID）
  • 精确位置（应用不请求定位）
  • 通讯录、麦克风；选图走系统相册选择器
  • 账号密码（应用无账号体系）
  • 回忆照片与笔记正文本身（不会作为分析事件内容上传）

2. 我们如何使用信息？

本地内容仅用于在你的设备上提供蜜迹功能：展示时光轴、特殊日提醒、回收站、备份导出等。

你主动发送的反馈邮件，仅用于改进产品与回复你。

Firebase 崩溃与分析数据仅用于稳定性与产品改进。

3. 共享与第三方

  • 我们不向广告商出售你们的回忆与照片。
  • Google Firebase（Crashlytics / Analytics）作为处理方接收诊断与事件数据；Facebook SDK 仅可选用于分享对话框（见上文）。
  • 系统相册选择器、通知、邮件、分享面板由操作系统或你选择的应用提供，受其各自政策约束。

4. 权限说明

权限用途
照片 / 媒体（通过系统选择器）由你挑选照片收进某一天；并非默认可读取全部相册
通知仅在你开启「本地提醒」后，在特殊日当天发送本机通知
振动等提升交互反馈

你可以在系统设置中随时关闭权限。关闭通知后，提醒功能将不可用，但不影响浏览已有回忆。

5. 数据保留与删除

  • 本地数据：由你控制。可在应用内删除单日回忆、清空回收站，或卸载应用。
  • 备份文件：由你保管；删除备份文件即可。
  • 反馈邮件：我们可能在合理期限内保留往来邮件以便处理请求，你可来信要求删除与反馈相关的邮件记录（不影响你设备上的本地相册）。

6. 儿童隐私

蜜迹面向情侣记录场景，不面向 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不明知收集儿童的个人信息。

7. 跨境与法律

因数据默认留在你的设备，不涉及我们主动将相册内容跨境传输到自有服务器。若你主动将备份或邮件发往境外服务，适用你所选服务的规则与当地法律。

8. 政策更新

若政策有实质变更，我们会更新本页生效日期，并视情况通过应用商店更新说明或应用内提示告知。继续使用即表示你了解更新后的政策。

9. 联系我们

App name: Mijii (蜜迹)

Mijii is designed so that your memories and photos stay on your device by default. We do not operate a cloud backend that syncs your couple album.

1. What information is involved?

1.1 On-device content (not uploaded to us)

The following stays in the app sandbox / local database on your phone and is not automatically uploaded to our servers (we do not provide such a backend):

  • Couple nicknames and the date you began
  • Memory dates, place/theme, mood tags, notes
  • Photos you choose to keep in Mijii, and thumbnails
  • Special-day settings and local reminder preferences
  • Language and similar UI preferences

Uninstalling the app or clearing app data typically removes this local content unless you exported a backup first.

1.2 Feedback you send

If you use in-app feedback and send email via your mail app, that message is handled by your email provider. We only read what you voluntarily send. This does not grant us access to your Mijii album.

1.3 Backup & share

Export creates a zip of the full database and all photos under your control. Where it goes (Files, AirDrop, etc.) is your choice. We do not upload backups to a Mijii cloud.

If you use Share, Mijii only renders a poster image on your device. You may send it via the system share sheet, save it to Photos, or (once developer credentials are configured) Facebook’s official Share Dialog. Mijii does not post on your behalf and does not upload share images to our servers. Once you send an image to a third-party app, that app’s privacy policy applies. The app may link the Facebook SDK solely for that optional Share Dialog; automatic app events and advertiser ID collection are disabled. It is not used for analytics or ads.

1.4 Crash diagnostics & usage analytics (Firebase)

To fix crashes and understand feature usage, the app uses Google Firebase:

  • Crashlytics: crash stacks, device model, OS version, app version, and similar diagnostics.
  • Analytics: anonymous/pseudonymous events such as app open, memory saved, poster share, backup export, and review prompts (no nicknames, note text, photo bytes, or file paths).

Google processes this data on our behalf over encrypted channels. We do not use it for personalized ads and do not sell your memories or photos. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

1.5 What we do not collect

We do not collect for advertising profiles or cross-app tracking:

  • Advertising IDs for tracking (we do not request ATT / ad ID for that purpose)
  • Precise location
  • Contacts or microphone; photos are selected via the system picker
  • Account passwords (there is no account system)
  • Memory photo bytes or note text as analytics event payloads

2. How information is used

On-device content is used only to provide Mijii features on your device (timeline, reminders, recycle bin, backup).

Feedback emails are used only to improve the product and reply to you.

Firebase crash and analytics data are used only for stability and product improvement.

3. Sharing & third parties

  • We do not sell your memories/photos to advertisers.
  • Google Firebase (Crashlytics / Analytics) processes diagnostics and events as a processor; Facebook SDK is optional for Share Dialog only (see above).
  • System photo picker, notifications, mail, and share sheet are provided by the OS or apps you choose.

4. Permissions

PermissionPurpose
Photos / media (via system picker)You pick photos to tuck into a day—not blanket library access by default
NotificationsOnly after you enable local reminders for special days
VibrationInteraction feedback

You can revoke permissions in system settings. Turning off notifications disables reminders but not browsing existing memories.

5. Retention & deletion

  • Local data: Under your control—delete days, empty the recycle bin, or uninstall.
  • Backup files: Stored where you put them; delete the files to remove them.
  • Feedback email: We may keep correspondence for a reasonable time to handle requests; email us to ask deletion of feedback-related records (this does not erase the album on your device).

6. Children

Mijii is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. International transfers

Because album data stays on your device by default, we do not actively transfer your album to our own servers. If you send a backup or email through a third-party service, that service’s terms and laws apply.

8. Changes

We will update the effective date when this policy materially changes, and may note changes in store release notes or in-app. Continued use means you understand the updated policy.

Nama aplikasi: Mijii (蜜迹)

Selamat datang di Mijii. Kebijakan ini menjelaskan bagaimana kami memperlakukan informasi saat Anda memakai aplikasi. Desain inti Mijii: kenangan dan foto secara default hanya tersimpan di perangkat Anda; kami tidak mengoperasikan server cloud untuk menyinkronkan album kalian.

1. Informasi apa yang kami kumpulkan?

1.1 Diproses di perangkat, tidak diunggah kepada kami

Konten yang Anda buat atau impor di aplikasi disimpan secara lokal (sandbox aplikasi dan basis data lokal) dan tidak otomatis diunggah ke server kami:

  • Nama panggilan pasangan dan tanggal mulai bersama
  • Tanggal kenangan, tempat / tema, suasana, catatan
  • Foto yang Anda pilih beserta thumbnail-nya
  • Pengaturan hari spesial dan sakelar pengingat lokal
  • Preferensi bahasa dan pengaturan antarmuka

Menghapus instalasi atau membersihkan data aplikasi biasanya menghapus konten lokal tersebut (kecuali Anda sudah mengekspor cadangan).

1.2 Masukan yang Anda kirim secara sukarela

Jika Anda mengirim email lewat fitur umpan balik, isi email diproses menurut aturan penyedia email Anda. Kami hanya membaca apa yang Anda tulis secara sukarela dan tidak mendapat akses ke album foto kalian karena itu.

1.3 Cadangan & berbagi

Ekspor cadangan membuat zip berisi basis data dan foto di bawah kendali Anda. Apakah file itu meninggalkan perangkat ditentukan oleh Anda; kami tidak mengunggahnya ke cloud Mijii.

Fitur berbagi hanya membuat gambar poster di perangkat. Anda dapat memakai berbagi sistem, menyimpan ke galeri, atau dialog berbagi Facebook resmi. Mijii tidak memposting atas nama Anda dan tidak mengunggah gambar berbagi ke server kami. Aplikasi dapat menautkan Facebook SDK hanya untuk dialog berbagi yang Anda picu; pengumpulan peristiwa otomatis dan ID pengiklan dinonaktifkan.

1.4 Diagnostik & analitik (Firebase)

Untuk memperbaiki crash dan memahami penggunaan fitur, aplikasi memakai Google Firebase:

  • Crashlytics: stack crash, model perangkat, versi OS, versi aplikasi.
  • Analytics: nama peristiwa semu-anonim (buka app, simpan kenangan, bagikan poster, ekspor cadangan, permintaan rating) tanpa nickname, isi catatan, atau jalur foto.

Google memproses data ini atas nama kami lewat saluran terenkripsi. Kami tidak memakai data itu untuk iklan bertarget dan tidak menjual kenangan atau foto kalian.

1.5 Yang tidak kami kumpulkan

  • ID iklan untuk pelacakan lintas aplikasi
  • Lokasi akurat
  • Kontak atau mikrofon; pemilihan foto lewat pemilih sistem
  • Kata sandi akun (tidak ada sistem akun)
  • Isi foto kenangan atau teks catatan sebagai muatan analitik

2. Bagaimana informasi digunakan?

Konten lokal hanya untuk fitur Mijii di perangkat Anda. Email umpan balik hanya untuk memperbaiki produk dan membalas Anda. Data Firebase hanya untuk stabilitas dan perbaikan produk.

3. Berbagi dengan pihak ketiga

  • Kami tidak menjual kenangan atau foto kalian kepada pengiklan.
  • Google Firebase menerima diagnostik dan peristiwa; Facebook SDK opsional hanya untuk dialog berbagi.
  • Pemilih foto sistem, notifikasi, email, dan panel berbagi tunduk pada kebijakan masing-masing.

4. Izin

IzinTujuan
Foto / media (pemilih sistem)Anda memilih foto untuk suatu hari; bukan akses penuh ke seluruh album secara default
NotifikasiHanya jika pengingat lokal diaktifkan, pada hari spesial
GetaranUmpan balik interaksi

5. Retensi & penghapusan

  • Data lokal: Anda kendalikan—hapus kenangan, kosongkan keranjang sampah, atau uninstall.
  • File cadangan: Anda simpan sendiri.
  • Email umpan balik: dapat kami simpan dalam jangka wajar; Anda dapat meminta penghapusan catatan email terkait.

6. Privasi anak

Mijii untuk catatan pasangan, tidak dirancang untuk anak di bawah 13 tahun, dan kami tidak sengaja mengumpulkan data pribadi anak.

7. Lintas batas & hukum

Karena data default tetap di perangkat, kami tidak secara aktif mengirim isi album ke server milik kami. Jika Anda mengirim cadangan atau email ke layanan luar negeri, aturan layanan itu dan hukum setempat berlaku.

8. Pembaruan kebijakan

Jika kebijakan berubah secara substantif, kami memperbarui tanggal berlaku di halaman ini. Penggunaan lanjutan berarti Anda memahami kebijakan yang diperbarui.

Bem-vindos ao Mijii. Esta política explica como tratamos informações ao usar o app. O desenho central do Mijii: memórias e fotos ficam, por padrão, só no seu aparelho; não operamos um servidor na nuvem para sincronizar o álbum de vocês.

1. Quais informações coletamos?

1.1 Processadas no aparelho, sem upload para nós

O conteúdo que vocês criam ou importam fica no aparelho (sandbox e banco local) e não é enviado automaticamente aos nossos servidores:

  • Apelidos do casal e a data em que começaram
  • Datas de memórias, lugar / tema, humor, notas
  • Fotos escolhidas e miniaturas
  • Dias especiais e lembretes locais
  • Preferência de idioma e ajustes da interface

Desinstalar o app ou limpar os dados geralmente remove esse conteúdo local (salvo se houver backup exportado).

1.2 Feedback enviado por vocês

Se enviarem e-mail pelo recurso de feedback, o conteúdo segue as regras do provedor de e-mail. Lemos só o que vocês escrevem voluntariamente e não ganhamos acesso ao álbum de fotos por causa disso.

1.3 Backup e compartilhamento

A exportação cria um zip com o banco e as fotos sob o controle de vocês. Se o arquivo sai do aparelho depende de vocês; não o enviamos para uma nuvem do Mijii.

O compartilhamento só gera um pôster no aparelho. Vocês podem usar a folha do sistema, salvar nas Fotos ou o diálogo oficial do Facebook. O Mijii não posta por vocês e não envia a imagem aos nossos servidores. O app pode incluir o Facebook SDK só para esse diálogo; eventos automáticos e ID de anunciante estão desativados.

1.4 Diagnósticos e análise (Firebase)

Para corrigir falhas e entender o uso, o app usa Google Firebase:

  • Crashlytics: pilha de crash, modelo do aparelho, versão do SO, versão do app.
  • Analytics: nomes de eventos pseudoanônimos (abertura, salvar memória, compartilhar pôster, exportar backup, pedido de avaliação), sem apelidos, texto de notas ou caminhos de foto.

O Google processa esses dados em nosso nome por canais criptografados. Não usamos para anúncios personalizados nem vendemos memórias ou fotos.

1.5 O que não coletamos

  • IDs de publicidade para rastreamento entre apps
  • Localização precisa
  • Contatos ou microfone; fotos via seletor do sistema
  • Senhas de conta (não há contas)
  • Conteúdo de fotos ou notas como carga de analytics

2. Como usamos as informações?

O conteúdo local serve só às funções do Mijii no aparelho. E-mails de feedback servem para melhorar o produto e responder. Dados do Firebase servem só à estabilidade e melhoria.

3. Compartilhamento com terceiros

  • Não vendemos memórias ou fotos a anunciantes.
  • O Google Firebase recebe diagnósticos e eventos; o Facebook SDK é opcional só para o diálogo de compartilhamento.
  • Seletor de fotos, notificações, e-mail e compartilhamento do sistema seguem as políticas respectivas.

5. Retenção e exclusão

  • Dados locais: sob controle de vocês—excluam memórias, esvaziem a lixeira ou desinstalem.
  • Arquivos de backup: ficam com vocês.
  • E-mails de feedback: podemos guardar por prazo razoável; vocês podem pedir a exclusão desses registros.

6. Privacidade de crianças

O Mijii é para o registro de casais, não é feito para menores de 13 anos, e não coletamos de propósito dados de crianças.

7. Fronteiras e lei

Como os dados ficam no aparelho por padrão, não transferimos ativamente o conteúdo do álbum para servidores nossos. Se enviarem backup ou e-mail a serviços no exterior, valem as regras desses serviços e a lei local.

8. Atualizações da política

Se houver mudança material, atualizamos a data nesta página. Continuar usando significa que vocês entendem a política atualizada.
